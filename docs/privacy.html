Privacy Policy for Irisa

Last updated: September 2026

In short

Irisa does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app has no internet connection, no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Data on your device

Game progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. They never leave it and we cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Sharing

When you share a result, your system's share sheet opens. You decide what to send and where; Irisa itself transmits nothing.

App stores

Apple and Google process data under their own privacy policies when you download the app.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technically necessary access data such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.
